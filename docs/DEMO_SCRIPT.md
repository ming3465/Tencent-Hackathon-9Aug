# Kampung SG 90-Second Demo Script

This script is a draft until final Miora assets, deployment, and playtest
findings are available. Replace every bracketed item before recording.

## Demo Goal

In 90 seconds, show a memorable Singapore neighbourhood, one complete resident
choice with a visible consequence, the optional memory table, the evening
payoff, and verifiable CodeBuddy and Miora creation evidence.

## Timed Script

### 0:00-0:08 - Emotional Hook

**Visual:** final title illustration, then the player entering the HDB estate.

**Narration:**

> What if ageing well was not about being looked after, but about still shaping
> the place you call home? This is Kampung SG.

### 0:08-0:18 - Sandbox Promise

**Visual:** move through the void deck, sheltered walk, hawker corner, and
community garden. Briefly open the Journal.

**Narration:**

> It is a cozy Singapore neighbourhood sandbox where families can explore one
> shared space and choose which invitations to join in any order.

### 0:18-0:36 - Resident Contribution and Choice

**Visual:** approach [BEST RESIDENT], open dialogue, choose [BEST CHOICE], and
hold on the visible world consequence and meter update.

**Narration:**

> Our older residents are not quest problems. They are the experts. [RESIDENT]
> contributes [EXPERTISE], and the player's choice immediately changes the
> neighbourhood.

### 0:36-0:47 - Optional Shared Activity

**Visual:** move to the memory table, match one pair, then cut to completion.

**Narration:**

> A quiet matching table is available as one optional shared activity. There is
> no timer, no medical score, and no claim to treat or prevent dementia.

### 0:47-0:59 - Evening Payoff

**Visual:** show three completed Journal items and open the evening reflection.

**Narration:**

> After three small acts, the void deck gathering reflects the choices the
> player actually made: connection, purpose, and everyday comfort made visible.

### 0:59-1:14 - AI Creation Evidence

**Visual:** split screen with CodeBuddy prompt and code/test output, then
procedural-before and Miora-after assets with prompt evidence.

**Narration:**

> CodeBuddy helped build and test the game systems. Miora transformed our
> procedural prototype into a consistent visual world. We preserved every
> prompt, correction, test, and selected asset for a transparent human-AI
> workflow.

### 1:14-1:24 - Accessibility and Evidence

**Visual:** keyboard movement, touch controls, Journal shortcut, and one concise
playtest result slide.

**Narration:**

> The same experience works through keyboard, touch, or non-spatial Journal
> shortcuts, and [NUMBER] playtests directly informed [TOP CHANGE].

### 1:24-1:30 - Closing Line

**Visual:** strongest evening neighbourhood shot and project URL.

**Narration:**

> Kampung SG: every small act grows the kampung, and every neighbour still has
> something to contribute.

## Required Shots

- [ ] Final title screen.
- [ ] Smooth movement through at least three recognizable Singapore locations.
- [ ] Nearby interaction marker and resident name.
- [ ] One complete resident choice.
- [ ] The choice-specific world consequence before and after.
- [ ] Meter and Journal update.
- [ ] Memory-table pair and completion.
- [ ] Journal shortcut for accessibility.
- [ ] Evening reflection generated from actual choices.
- [ ] CodeBuddy exact prompt, changed code, and passing checks.
- [ ] Miora prompt, selected output, and integrated asset.
- [ ] Procedural-before and Miora-after comparison.
- [ ] One truthful playtest finding and resulting change.
- [ ] Public URL and project name.

## Recording Rules

- Record the deployed production build, not the development server.
- Use a deterministic route and resident choice for every take.
- Keep the mouse cursor away from focal content unless demonstrating controls.
- Record clean game audio and narration separately when possible.
- Add captions and verify spelling of resident names and tool names.
- Do not accelerate footage so much that older-user interaction appears
  unnaturally fast.
- Do not show unapproved Miora generations, secrets, account details, or private
  CodeBuddy data.
- Do not claim older-adult testing, Miora usage, deployment, or impact until it
  actually occurred.

## Live Demo Backup

- Start from a fresh title screen in a private browser window.
- Use the Journal shortcut if movement or live presentation timing fails.
- Demonstrate one resident choice and one visible world consequence.
- Skip the memory table if time is below 45 seconds.
- Open the evening through a prepared three-activity route.
- Keep a local production build and final video available if the network fails.
