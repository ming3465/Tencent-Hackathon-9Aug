# Research and Claim Guardrails

## Safe Product Claim

Kampung SG is an accessible social memory game intended to encourage shared
play, conversation, and positive engagement with Singapore stories.

## Prohibited Claims

Do not state or imply that the game diagnoses cognitive impairment, measures
cognitive age, prevents dementia, delays dementia, treats dementia, improves
general health, or produces guaranteed cognitive benefits.

## Evidence Position

Cognitive games can improve performance on practiced tasks in some studies,
but transfer to everyday functioning and dementia prevention is uncertain.
Enjoyment and acceptance also vary among older adults. Product evaluation must
therefore focus on usability, engagement, accessibility, and observed social
interaction rather than clinical outcomes.

## Phase 1 Measurement

Phase 1 records no participant data. Automated tests measure software
correctness only. Future playtests may record anonymous manual observations
only after a separate consent and privacy plan is approved.
