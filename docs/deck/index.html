The Loop

A place, not a menu.

  • 1600×1000 estate: HDB block, void deck, hawker corner, garden
  • Move with WASD, arrow keys, or the on-screen touch pad
  • Approach a resident, press E, choose together
  • Three meters rise: Connection, Purpose, Comfort
  • Any three activities unlock the evening reflection
  • No timer. No energy bar. No failure state.
1. WalkDiscover activities in any order you like
2. ChooseBoth options are valid preferences
3. The world changesYour choice appears in the estate

AI Creation Workflow

How AI built this, honestly.

Every team will say their AI tool worked great. Ours didn't — the first time. That is why we can show you a verification gate instead of a promise.

AI produced What our gate caught What we did Verified after
CodeBuddy CLI 2.127.0 authored the first 10 files, including the match engine that still ships today Overran its 50-turn limit. 2 TypeScript errors · 3 failing tests · 1 unused dependency · 4 audit findings · 1 stale-timer race Rewrote tests off an invalid constant-RNG shuffle assumption; added session-aware timer cancellation tsc clean · 65/65 tests · 0 vulnerabilities · 21/21 browser smoke
When we asked CodeBuddy to fix its own output, the account returned 429 Credits exhausted. We did not buy credits, and we did not invent extra runs to make this slide look better. We treat AI output as a pull request, not a deliverable.

Human decided: that residents are experts not quest problems; the no-medical-claims policy, written before any code; non-punitive choices.

AI produced: the Phaser world, movement, collision, camera and HUD; the deterministic domain logic and its 65 tests; the Web Audio synthesis layer.

  • Vite + TypeScript + Phaser 3, strict mode throughout
  • Engine lazy-loads only after “Begin” — matters on an older phone

06

Integrity

What we tested. What we won't claim.

What we can support

  • Strict TypeScript, 65 passing tests, 0 vulnerabilities
  • 21/21 production-browser checks, including a second playthrough
  • Completable by keyboard, touch, or Journal shortcuts alone
  • No data collection, no account, no network dependency
  • Evening reflection built from the player's real choices

What we deliberately do not claim

  • Any cognitive, memory, or dementia benefit
  • Any clinical or health outcome
  • Any measured user or population result
  • Any claim about what this does for a real person's life
We wrote our claim guardrails before we wrote the game. Kampung SG does not diagnose, measure, prevent, delay, or treat anything. On a health-adjacent topic, that restraint is the design — not a limitation of it.
