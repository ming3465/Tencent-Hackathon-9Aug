The Campaign

Five doors tell one connected story.

  • Prologue, three sequential chapters, one ending, then free exploration
  • 2560×1600 estate plus homes, corridor, workshop, shops and halls
  • Move with WASD, arrow keys, or the on-screen touch pad
  • Recruit 3 helpers, invite 5 residents, reconnect Ben with a craftsman
  • Circular map opens Places; quest Journal tracks every objective
  • No timer. No energy bar. No failure state.
1. EnterHomes and landmarks carry resident-specific details
2. ListenKampungMind selects authored intent from context
3. RememberChoices persist in people, places and the estate

The Residents

Eight routes. One private KampungMind.

Provision Shop

Auntie Minah

Everyday scam-safety expert. She teaches neighbours to pause, verify through a trusted source, and never share an OTP.

Void-Deck Noticeboard

Uncle Ravi

The void-deck host. He turns a board full of notices into an invitation, and welcomes chess beginners.

Shaded Route

Mdm Siti

Route expert. She maps sun and rain exposure from walking it daily, and her knowledge drives the shelter plan.

  • Personality, role, expertise, knowledge and memory rules per NPC
  • Numbered steps or icons + words: both advance; no test or penalty
  • The selected CHECK FIRST card stays in Minah's shop window

06

Integrity

What we tested. What we won't claim.

What we can support

  • Strict TypeScript, 90 passing tests, 0 vulnerabilities
  • 60/60 production-browser checks across full and demo campaigns
  • Keyboard and touch entry, focus return and correct return doors
  • No data collection, no account, no network dependency
  • Versioned autosave, Continue, confirmed Start Over and demo isolation

What we deliberately do not claim

  • Any cognitive, memory, or dementia benefit
  • Any clinical or health outcome
  • Any measured user or population result
  • Any claim about what this does for a real person's life
We wrote our claim guardrails before we wrote the game. Kampung SG does not diagnose, measure, prevent, delay, or treat anything. On a health-adjacent topic, that restraint is the design — not a limitation of it.
