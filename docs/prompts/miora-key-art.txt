PROMPT 1 - MASTER KEY ART (generate this first; 16:9)
--------------------------------------------------------------------

Warm flat-design illustration of a Singapore HDB housing estate at golden
hour. A cream apartment block with a coral-red roof edge, rows of square
windows - some glowing warm gold - with bamboo laundry poles jutting from the
windows, colourful clothes drying. Open void deck on pillars at ground level.
In the foreground, a dignified silver-haired Singaporean elder holding a
walking cane stands mid-conversation with a younger visitor on a sandy
footpath; both relaxed, warm, equals. Around them: a broad flat-crowned rain
tree, a community garden bed with neat soil rows and green seedlings, a small
wooden bench.

Style: cozy storybook flat illustration with bold, uniform deep-teal ink
outlines (#173F4F) around every shape, simplified readable geometry, subtle
grain texture. Strict palette: deep teal #173F4F, warm cream #FFF6DC, coral
#D96756, golden yellow #F2B84B, community teal #287271, garden green #5B8C5A,
soft purple #775B91, concrete sand #EAD9B7. Light from the upper left, long
soft golden-hour glow. Calm, welcoming, lived-in, distinctly Singaporean
without tourist cliches.

No text, no lettering, no signage with words, no logo, no watermark, no UI,
no photorealism, no 3D render, no anime, no caricature, no hospital or
medical imagery, no frailty stereotypes - the elder is confident and at ease.

--------------------------------------------------------------------

- The elder reads as dignified and active - reject anything frail, hunched,

- No baked-in text or watermark anywhere (zoom in and check corners).
- Light comes from the upper left.
- Looks like OUR game's world (laundry poles, void deck, rain tree), not a
