## Exact prompt

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: game environment density and prop-cluster reference for a playable top-down pixel RPG
Primary request: Create a new, original Singapore HDB-estate pixel-art environment concept that demonstrates how to make the open lawns and plazas in the three reference screenshots feel intentionally dense, lived-in, layered, and cozy while preserving generous clear movement routes.
Input images: Image 1 is the current central estate gameplay; Image 2 is the current east district; Image 3 is the current south district. Use all three only as scale, palette, projection, and project-identity references. Do not edit or reproduce their exact composition.
Scene/backdrop: tropical Singapore HDB estate with a void deck, sheltered linkway, kopitiam edge, provision-shop edge, community-centre edge, grass pockets, concrete paths, drains, and mature planting.
Subject: show six reusable environmental story clusters integrated naturally: a stone chess table with fixed stools; a bicycle rack beside planters; a compact maintenance trolley with broom and bucket; a utility box with hose reel and drain; a tidy stack of community chairs beside a noticeboard; and a shaded seating planter with fallen leaves. Style/medium: crisp original pixel art designed to be translated into deterministic Phaser canvas shapes; coherent 32-pixel tile logic; limited warm cream, teal, coral, gold, grass green, purple, and deep navy palette; hard pixel edges; strong readable silhouettes; consistent 3/4 top-down projection; 3-to-5-pixel dark outlines; layered cast shadows and lit upper-left edges.
Composition/framing: wide playable-scene concept at the same apparent object scale as the references; distribute density in clusters at path edges and building thresholds; maintain two unmistakably clear primary travel lanes and all door approaches.
Lighting/mood: humid tropical morning, friendly and active, directional shade under trees and shelters.
Constraints: no UI, no dialogue box, no labels, no readable text, no logos, no watermark, no copied game characters or copyrighted map designs, no direct imitation of Stardew Valley; code-drawable geometry and restrained detail; every entrance and movement lane remains clear.
Avoid: painterly rendering, smooth anti-aliased illustration, fantasy-farm motifs, pseudo-writing, oversized props, crowded sidewalks, medical imagery, stereotypes, empty featureless lawns.
```

## Human review

Accepted as visual-direction evidence:

- density grouped into readable story clusters instead of uniform clutter;
- stone chess seating that signals ordinary community expertise;
- bicycles framed by planters rather than floating on open paving;
- maintenance trolley, utility box, hose reel, and drain as one service cluster;
- stacked community chairs beside a noticeboard;
- shaded seating integrated with planting;
- drain grates, leaf scatter, and tiny insects as low-cost environmental motion;
- clear central routes and door approaches despite higher density.

Rejected for direct runtime use:

- its sprite and foliage detail is substantially richer than the current
  deterministic canvas language;
- some noticeboard marks could read as generated pseudo-writing;
- generated residents do not match the authored resident silhouettes;
- the full raster would replace collision-aware, chapter-persistent scene
  state with a flat image.

Curated implementation decision:

- translate only the accepted cluster grammar into original code-drawn Phaser
  textures, placements, collisions, and deterministic ambient motion;
- keep entrances and the two main estate travel axes clear;
- freeze or hide new ambient motion under reduced motion and monsoon states;
- extend production smoke evidence for prop density, insect motion, collision,
  and frame budgets;
- do not label this run as Miora.
