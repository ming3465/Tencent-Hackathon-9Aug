Why the refusals matter. Our engine picks which line a resident says; the model only chooses the words. Left unguarded, this model rewrote shelter the walkway as fix the drainage — and that quest builds a sheltered walkway the game draws. So we measure how much of the meaning survives, and lines that carry a consequence must keep 80% of it. Anything below that is thrown away and the written line is used instead. A model that can be overruled is safer than one that cannot.
